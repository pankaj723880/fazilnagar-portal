## 2. Strict Town Boundary Methodology

In compliance with the **Non-Negotiable Geographical Rule**:
1. **Town Core Definition:** Fazilnagar town (historically and culturally known as Pawanagar) centers on the National Highway 27 corridor (former NH-28) intersected by College Road, Hospital Road, Babu Bazar (C.C. Road), Purani Basti Road, and Block Road.
2. **PIN Code Filtering:** Establishments possessing postal addresses with PIN `274401` were **not** automatically admitted. The development block covers numerous surrounding rural villages (such as Madanpur, Pataherwa proper, Baghauch, Pipra). Only establishments with proven physical presence within the town settlement footprint are published.
3. **Quarantine Procedure:** Candidates situated outside the town built-up perimeter or lacking physical verification are assigned `boundary_unverified` or `outside` status, `is_published: false`, and routed to the moderation review queue.
