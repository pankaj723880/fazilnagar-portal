Town Profile & History

A Historic Crossroad in Kushinagar District

Fazilnagar (historically identified with ancient Pawanagar or Pawa of the Malla republic) is a prominent commercial and educational town located along the Four-Lane National Highway 27 in Kushinagar district, Uttar Pradesh.

Situated approximately 20 km southeast of the Kushinagar Buddhist heritage circuit and International Airport, Fazilnagar connects Kasya with Tamkuhi Raj and Bihar. The town is revered in Jain tradition for Lord Mahavira's nirvana connection and is an important center for inter colleges, farming commerce, and local healthcare.
